Approve the V4 fee collector

One-time setup. Your wallet signs a single setApprovalForAll on the Uniswap v4 PositionManager. No private key is entered anywhere.

With this approval the operator can call modifyLiquidities on positions this wallet owns. The collector uses it only to take accrued fees (decrease 0 liquidity, then take both currencies to the sweep recipient). It cannot move tokens or ETH held in your wallet, and you can revoke it any time with setApprovalForAll(operator, false).
